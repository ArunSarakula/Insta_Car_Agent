# Best posting times for @decodethedrive (Indian audience, IST)

Researched 17 Oct 2026 from India-focused 2026 guides (WsCube Tech, Sociolyzer,
My Digital Sevak, StartUpMandi). They agree closely; none publish raw data, so
these are starting points until our own Insights take over.

| Day | Best window (IST) | Backup |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | 19:00-21:00 | 12:00-13:00 |
| Tue | 19:00-21:00 (strongest weekday) | 11:00-13:00 |
| Wed | 19:00-21:00 (strongest weekday) | 12:00-14:00 |
| Thu | 19:00-21:00 | 12:00-13:00 |
| Fri | 19:00-20:30 | 12:00-13:00 |
| Sat | 10:30-12:30 | 20:00-22:00 |
| Sun | 11:00-13:00 | 20:00-21:30 |

- Reels: weekday evenings 19:00-20:30 get the most reach.
- Avoid 23:00-07:00.
- Posts go out within 30 minutes of the planned time (the agent checks at :07 and :37).

## Switching to our own data

Once the account has 100 followers, Instagram shares when *our* followers are
online. The weekly report then lists those hours (IST), and the weekly plan
follows them instead of the table above.
