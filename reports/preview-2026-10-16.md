**Caption:**

"Level 2 ADAS" is on every brochure now, but what does it actually mean? 🤔

Quick version:
Level 0 = warnings only
Level 1 = one helper at a time (cruise OR steering)
Level 2 = the car can handle steering and speed together, but YOU are still the driver

No car on sale in India drives itself. ADAS is a co-pilot, not an autopilot. Treat it like a smart assistant that still needs supervision, just like that intern who's great but needs code review. 😄

## 2026-10-17 10:00 · reel · AC vs DC charging
Post id: `b80146a3`

<a href="../media/b80146a3/reel.mp4"><img src="../media/b80146a3/cover.jpg" width="220"></a> (tap for the video)

**Caption:**

AC charger, DC charger, onboard charger, kW... EV charging jargon can feel like a networking exam. ⚡

Here's the simple version:
Your battery stores DC. Grid power is AC. The only question is WHO converts it. If the car does it, that's AC charging (slower). If the station does it, that's DC fast charging (much quicker).
