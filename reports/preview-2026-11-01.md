## 2026-11-11 19:30 · reel · Regen braking myth: highway range

Music: alex-morgan-future-bass-545515.mp3 (upbeat)

YouTube Shorts title: **How does regenerative braking work in EVs?**
YouTube hashtags: #shorts #RegenerativeBraking #EV

**Caption:**

Regenerative braking turns your motor into a generator when you slow down, sending energy back to the battery. That's why EVs and strong hybrids love stop-go city traffic, and why it doesn't add much on a steady highway run.

One safety note: one-pedal or strong regen modes are great, but keep your foot ready for the brake pedal. Regen alone won't do an emergency stop.

YouTube hashtags: #shorts #TyrePressure #CarTips

**Caption:**

Most of us check air at the petrol pump right after a drive. Problem: hot tyres read higher, so we let air out and end up underinflated 🔥

The right number is on the sticker inside your driver's door (or in the owner's manual), not the max pressure printed on the tyre.
